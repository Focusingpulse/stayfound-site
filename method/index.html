A public explanation

The work is not more content. The work is a truer record.

Search engines and AI assistants do not experience the business. They assemble a version of it from sources. Stayfound works on the gap between the business and that assembled version.

The core distinction

Visibility is not the same as understanding.

NAMED

Are you in the answer?

Retrieval is the first question. If the business never appears, the buyer never gets to evaluate it.

DESCRIBED

Is the description accurate?

A wrong service, old location, or confused entity can make a mention actively harmful.

RECOMMENDED

Is the evidence trustworthy?

Recommendation depends on corroboration, not just the business's own page saying it is good.

VERIFIED

Can the claim survive a retest?

A change is not a result until the same question is run again and the new state is recorded.

What I will not do

I will not make the measurement look better than it is.

No invented proof.

  • No fabricated reviews or case studies.
  • No unsupported statistics.
  • No ranking guarantees.
  • No “not measured” converted into a zero or a win.

No activity for activity's sake.

  • No posts without a measured reason.
  • No citation expansion before identity repair.
  • No city-swapped pages.
  • No public claim without a source or an explicit label.
