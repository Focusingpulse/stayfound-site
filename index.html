The work

I do not start by writing more words. I start by finding the break.

A good answer is built from a chain of evidence. If the chain is broken, publishing another page can make the noise louder without making the business clearer.

01 / OBSERVE

Ask the real questions.

We test the prompts buyers actually use and record what each engine returns.

02 / TRACE

Find the evidence.

We identify the pages, listings, profiles, reviews, and data that shaped the answer.

03 / FIX

Repair the source.

We correct the entity, the structure, the citations, or the content. The fix follows the finding.

04 / VERIFY

Run it again.

A finding is not closed because a change was made. We check whether the same test comes back clean.

After the finding

The repair plan follows the break.

There is no universal “more SEO” button. The next action depends on what the record says and what the evidence can support.

If identity is wrong

Lock the entity.

Name, service, location, category, and relationships are made explicit before they are amplified.

If sources disagree

Reconcile the record.

Correct the profiles, citations, reviews, structured data, and pages that are teaching different versions of the business.

If the answer is thin

Build the missing evidence.

Create the useful page, proof, or buyer answer the record actually needs. Not content for its own sake.

If the answer changes

Verify the change.

Retest the same question, compare the answer, and leave an open item when the result is still uncertain.

Questions

The short answers.

What does Stayfound Optimized actually do?

I measure how AI assistants name, describe, and recommend a business. Then I trace the evidence behind those answers and fix the parts that are wrong, missing, or inconsistent.

Is this just SEO with a new name?

No. Search visibility still matters, but an AI answer is not a list of ten blue links. This work also looks at entity identity, third-party corroboration, structured information, reviews, and the sources an assistant uses when it forms an answer.

Can I check this myself first?

Yes. Use the self-check above. It is not a full audit, but it will show whether the problem is absence, inaccuracy, or weak evidence.

Who is this for?

Established businesses and mission-driven organizations whose reputation matters, especially when a buyer's recommendation depends on being understood correctly.

How long does the work take?

The Foundation Audit is a one-time engagement. Ongoing changes depend on what is wrong and where the evidence lives. Established signals can shift within 30 to 60 days, but a specific ranking promise next week would be guesswork.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Ongoing work is month to month. The goal is to earn its place by showing what changed and what was done.

What if I do not need help?

Then I will tell you. If your business is named, described correctly, and supported by good sources, you may not need Stayfound Optimized yet.
