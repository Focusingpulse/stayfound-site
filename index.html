Questions

The ones people actually ask.

What is AI answer visibility?

When someone asks an AI assistant for a recommendation, it gives one answer with a short list of names instead of ten links. Whether you're in that list, and whether the description is accurate, depends on signals the assistant can verify: consistent listings, structured data, reviews, and content that answers the question directly. That's what I manage.

Why do Apple and Bing matter, not just Google?

Siri and Copilot don't read Google. They read Apple Business Connect and Bing Places. A business that's perfect on Google and absent on the other two is invisible to a large share of voice and AI searches, and most owners have never been told.

How long until I see something?

Established signals can shift in 30 to 60 days. Real movement typically lands inside 90. Anyone promising you a specific ranking next week is guessing or lying.

What does "correct" mean, exactly?

It means the assistant describes you the way you'd describe yourself: right services, right area, right hours, no invented facts, no stale address, no competitor's details bleeding into your listing. Being named and being described correctly are two different problems, and the second one is harder.
