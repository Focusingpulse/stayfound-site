Questions

The ones people actually ask.

What does it mean to be cited by an AI engine?

When someone asks an AI assistant for a recommendation, it gives one answer with a short list of names instead of ten links. Being cited means your business appears in that answer. Being correct means the description attached to it is accurate. They are two different problems, and the second one is harder.

How do I check where I stand right now?

Ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity the question your customer would ask. Then ask each one your business name on its own and read what it says. Most owners are surprised twice: once that they aren't named, and once that what is written about them is wrong. Doing that across every engine is what the systems on this page do continuously.

Why do Apple and Bing matter, not just Google?

Siri and Copilot don't read Google. They read Apple Business Connect and Bing Places. A business that's perfect on Google and absent on the other two is invisible to a large share of voice and AI searches, and most owners have never been told.

How long until I see something?

Established signals can shift in 30 to 60 days. Real movement typically lands inside 90. Anything claiming a specific ranking next week is guessing or lying.
